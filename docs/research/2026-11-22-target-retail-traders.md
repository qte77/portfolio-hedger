# Target user: retail options traders, and the gap this leaves (2026-11-22)

Closes the research gap in #4 by choosing one of its options: the target is narrowed to self-directed retail options
traders, and the professional-buyer gap is recorded here rather than closed.

## Why this option

#4 listed four ways forward: talk to 3–5 professionals; search the venues not yet reached (Wilmott, QuantNet,
adviser outlets, tool reviews); register a Reddit API app; or narrow the target and record the gap. The first three
need either people or access this project does not have. The evidence it does have points one way:

- The decision-grade personas, [1 and 2](personas.md), are retail traders, built from public trader discussions in the
  [first evaluation](2026-11-02-persona-evaluation.md).
- The professional search ([ICP2 note](2026-11-02-icp2-professional-buyers.md)) supported only one advisory persona
  (5), with one pattern backed by two independent professional sources.
- What shipped since was checked against primary sources rather than buyers: the cash-account rules against Cboe and
  FINRA material ([cash rules note](2026-11-13-cash-rules-vs-cboe.md)), now including the Cboe Margin Manual itself.

## What changes

- [`docs/spec.md`](../spec.md) names the target user: a self-directed retail options trader hedging their own book.
- Product decisions are weighed against Personas 1 and 2. Personas 3–5 stay advisory, as before.
- The hedge-policy idea (#5) came from the professional sources. It was closed as not planned on 2026-11-25 (plan
  0005 row 3); reopen it if a real request arrives.

## The gap, kept on record

- **Not known:** whether registered investment advisers, prop traders or small funds would use the tool, and what
  they would need from it beyond Persona 5's thin grounding.
- **Not reached:** Reddit (blocked on every fetch tier), Wilmott, QuantNet, ThinkAdvisor, Financial Planning;
  Advisor Perspectives was blocked.
- **Reopen when:** a professional user reports through the app's feedback form or an issue, or the owner chooses to
  interview professionals or register a Reddit API app. Then rerun the professional search and Persona 5's criteria
  in a new dated note here.
